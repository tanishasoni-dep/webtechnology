Department of Computer Engineering

Our Department

The Department of Computer Engineering at CHARUSAT focuses on developing strong technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities and innovative thinking among students.

The department provides an environment where students can explore programming, software development, artificial intelligence, networking and emerging technologies.

Why Computer Engineering?

  • Industry-oriented education
  • Modern computer laboratories
  • Experienced faculty
  • Innovation and research
  • Career development opportunities
